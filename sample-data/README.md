# Sample data

Every file here is **invented**. The names, bank accounts (each IBAN contains `FAKE`), addresses and amounts do not belong to real people or companies. Each file has red flags planted on purpose so you can see what the tests find.

The files are generated by [`tools/make_sample_data.py`](../tools/make_sample_data.py). The automated checks in [`tests/`](../tests/) confirm that every planted red flag is found.

## benford-ap-payments.csv — Benford's Law

3,140 supplier payments. Most follow Benford's Law. Planted: **140 payments between €4,800 and €4,999 from Brightline Consulting**, just under a €5,000 approval limit. They show up as spikes at 48 and 49 in the first-two-digits test.

Also included and correctly **not** flagged: nine monthly rent invoices of €12,500 (listed under "Recurring amounts"), and a credit note.

## journal-entries.csv — Journal entry testing

1,374 entries (2,748 lines), January to September 2026. Most are automated (`AUTO`) postings. Planted manual entries:

- **JE261369**: Sunday 29 March at 23:47 by `admin01`, a user with no other entries. €85,000 from revenue to the suspense account, described "Reclass as instructed".
- **JE261370**: 30 June (quarter end) at 21:15 by `fin.director`. €250,000 revenue booked against accruals, "Revenue adjustment per CFO", **reversed** by JE261371 two days later.
- **JE261372**: 30 September at 19:40, €40,000 to the suspense account with no description.
- A Saturday entry for travel expenses.
- One entry that does not balance (€45 difference), so the file total does not balance either.

To run the unusual-account-combination test on this file, set *Revenue accounts start with* to `4` and *Normal accounts against revenue start with* to `10, 11`.
