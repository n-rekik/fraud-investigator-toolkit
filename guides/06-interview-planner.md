# 06. Interview planner

Turns your case facts into an interview plan: who to interview, in what order, and which questions and documents to use with each person.

**Status:** prompt guide. This project runs in an AI assistant today; a built-in tool may follow. [Contributions welcome](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## What you need

A short case summary, the list of people involved with their roles, and a list of your key documents. Replace real names with codes such as Witness A and Subject 1.

## Prompt to copy

Replace the parts in [SQUARE BRACKETS] with your own details.

```text
You are helping a Certified Fraud Examiner plan interviews. Here is the case summary: [PASTE].
People involved (coded): [LIST WITH ROLES]. Key documents: [LIST WITH EXHIBIT NUMBERS].

1. Propose an interview order, starting with neutral third-party witnesses, then corroborating witnesses, then possible co-conspirators, and the subject last. Give the reason for each position.
2. For each interview write: the objective, 3 introductory questions, 8 to 12 open information-gathering questions, and the documents to show and when.
3. For the subject interview, list the facts I must be able to prove with documents before I hold it.
4. List the gaps: facts I still need and who could provide them.
Use open, non-accusatory questions. Do not write leading questions.
```

## Check before you trust it

- You decide the order and the questions. The plan is a draft to improve with your own knowledge of the people.
- Check your organisation's rules and local law on employee interviews, including who must be present and what notice is required.
- Keep real names out of the AI tool unless it is approved for that data.

## Before you upload anything

- **Check permission first.** Case data is confidential. Only use an AI tool your organisation has approved for that data.
- **Remove what you do not need.** Delete or mask names, bank details and ID numbers when the test works without them.
- **Practise on sample data.** Build and test on a fake or old file first.
