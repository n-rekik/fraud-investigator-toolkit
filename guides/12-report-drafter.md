# 12. Investigation report drafter

Turns your working notes into a structured first draft of the investigation report, with every finding tied to an exhibit.

**Status:** prompt guide. This project runs in an AI assistant today; a built-in tool may follow. [Contributions welcome](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## What you need

Your notes, the chronology from guide 07, your exhibit list and your organisation's report template if it has one.

## Prompt to copy

Replace the parts in [SQUARE BRACKETS] with your own details.

```text
You are helping a Certified Fraud Examiner draft an investigation report from the attached notes, chronology and exhibit list.

Use this structure: 1. Executive summary. 2. Background. 3. Allegation and scope. 4. Approach (work performed). 5. Findings. 6. Summary of loss. 7. Recommendations. 8. List of exhibits.

Rules:
- State facts only. Give no opinion on the guilt or innocence of any person.
- Never use the words fraud, theft, guilty or criminal about a person. Describe what the documents and witnesses show.
- Every finding must cite an exhibit number or an interview. If my notes give no source, write [SOURCE NEEDED].
- If my notes do not cover something, write [TO COMPLETE]. Do not fill gaps with assumptions.
- Use short sentences, past tense and neutral language. Use the codes for people as they appear in my notes.

After the draft, list every [SOURCE NEEDED] and [TO COMPLETE] item, and any statement in my notes that contradicts another.
```

## Check before you trust it

- Read every sentence against your evidence. You sign the report, so every fact must be yours.
- Check that the loss figure adds up and matches the exhibits.
- Follow your normal review process, including legal review, before the report goes to anyone.

## Before you upload anything

- **Check permission first.** Case data is confidential. Only use an AI tool your organisation has approved for that data.
- **Remove what you do not need.** Delete or mask names, bank details and ID numbers when the test works without them.
- **Practise on sample data.** Build and test on a fake or old file first.
